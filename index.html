01 — Who we are

From formwork to full automation.

Midland Steel is Ireland's leading offsite reinforcement steel supplier, working across the Irish and UK construction markets from our headquarters in Mountmellick, Co. Laois.

We started out in 1990 as Midland Formwork, before founding Midland Steel in 1998. Today we're a team of engineers, BIM modellers, robotic technicians and steel fixers who design and manufacture bespoke rebar solutions using cloud-based 3D modelling — and we were the first reinforcement steel company in the world to reach BIM Level 2 (ISO 19650 Tier 2) status.

02 — Innovation

Robotics, BIM, and offsite manufacturing.

Working at Midland Steel means working alongside modern methods of construction, not just reading about them.

Product

FasterFix

Our patented, offsite manufactured modular reinforcement system takes rebar from BIM model to site-ready cage without the traditional on-site fixing bottleneck.

Facility

SMART Portlaoise

A fully automated cutting and bending facility, opened with a €16 million investment, giving our technicians and engineers a genuinely modern floor to work on.

Process

Cloud-based BIM

Full 3D modelling to ISO 19650 Tier 2 status means our BIM modellers and estimators collaborate on projects of any scale, from design straight through to placement.
